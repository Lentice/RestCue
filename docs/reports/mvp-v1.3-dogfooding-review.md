- **Duration**: Initial Day 1 baseline only. Full 5-workday cycle not completed.
- **Subjective ratings**: Disruption and fatigue are self-reported 1–5 scales for

- **No generalizations**: Results from this dogfooding cannot be extrapolated to
  other users, hardware, or usage patterns.
